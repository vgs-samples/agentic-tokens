Our story

Three generations.
One stubborn idea: grow slow.

My grandfather planted our first row of Bluecrop bushes in 1962. We still hand-pick every single berry — no mechanical shakers, no plastic clamshells from a packing line in another state. Just our family, a couple dozen neighbors each season, and fruit that ripens on the branch until it's almost too sweet to ship.

Then we put it in a paper pulp box, surround it with a cold pack, and send it your way. That's the whole trick.

Questions

The fine print, but honest.

What if my berries don't arrive in great shape? +

Send a photo within 48 hours and we'll re-ship or refund — no inspection visits, no forms. Once or twice a year a box gets stuck in a hot truck. It happens; we fix it.

Where do you ship? +

All 48 contiguous US states. Alaska and Hawaii coming summer 2026 — drop your email above to be notified.

Is it really a year-round subscription? +

Our peak season runs May–September. October through April we ship from our partner co-op of small organic farms in the Southern Hemisphere — same standards, same humans, no industrial shortcuts.

Can I pause, skip, or cancel? +

Yes, all of the above, anytime, from your account. Two clicks. We don't believe in retention dark patterns.

What varieties will I get? +

Over the year you'll taste Bluecrop, Chandler, Liberty, Aurora, Elliott, Pink Lemonade, and our heirloom Cascade Wild. Each box includes a card describing what's inside and how to use it.
